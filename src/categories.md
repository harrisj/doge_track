There is a lot that we don't know about DOGE's operations. In contrast to their rhetoric about transparency, they continue to operate in relative obscurity, with multiple teams focused on different major projects at various agencies. To help myself keep track of the various people, I have found it useful to sort the DOGE staff into multiple categories that reflect their main function at DOGE. These will be expanded on in subsequent pages.

<p><ul>
<li><strong>Boosters</strong> are people in DOGE's orbit who have helped with recruitment and its formation</li>
<li><strong>Enablers</strong> are staff embedded in agencies who work to open the door for wreckers to come in. They are not always DOGE hires.</li>
<li><strong>Leadership</strong> are the identified leaders of the operation</li>
<li><strong>Support</strong> are the home team, largely based in DOGE and OPM and there to support operations</li>
<li><strong>Wreckers</strong> are the away team and the muscle, dispatched to descend on agencies to infiltrate operations, exfiltrate data and possibly destroy entirely</li>
</ul></p>

As always, any categorization is an approximation. Reality is slightly more nuanced than this model. For instance, people might change their roles over time - _e.g_, Scott Coulter went from being a wrecker at NASA to an enabler CIO at SSA - but I only classify them in one category.
